One line, at most 20 words, that a gamer texts the boss to get out of work during GTA 6 launch week — written deterministically to `/output/entry.txt` for make.fun's "The Excuse That Almost Works" bounty. A boring, specific, believable home emergency (boiler burst at 3am, plumber till noon, kitchen ankle-deep), chosen by pairwise judging of eight variants against the judge's brief.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Outside the grader, set `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` to choose where `entry.txt` is written (default `/output`).
